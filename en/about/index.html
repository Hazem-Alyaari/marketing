What it is

One platform for connected school operations

MySchool is a multi-tenant school management platform that brings academic, administrative, financial, HR, supervision, and portal experiences into one system.

Schools use it to organize day-to-day work — from admissions and student records to attendance, fees, employee processes, and family communication — with clear access for each role.

Why it exists

School work is connected — tools often are not.

When admissions, academics, finance, HR, and family communication live in separate places, teams spend more time reconciling information than running the school. MySchool exists to keep those operations together while respecting role boundaries.

Product principles

How MySchool approaches school operations

These principles come from how the product is built — not from marketing slogans alone.

  • Connected operations

    Admissions, academics, attendance, finance, HR, supervision, and communication belong in one school system instead of scattered tools.

  • Role-based experiences

    Management, teachers, supervisors, finance, HR, students, and guardians each get an experience suited to their work.

  • Centralized school data

    Student, academic, financial, and staffing information is organized in one school workspace so teams follow the same operational picture.

  • Clear permissions

    Access is controlled by roles and permissions — so users see what their responsibilities require, not everything in the school.

  • Arabic and English

    MySchool supports Arabic and English interfaces so school teams can work in the language that fits their context.

  • Branch-aware structure

    When a school needs it, branch management helps organize multi-branch operations within the same platform.

Access & trust

Separation by responsibility — not one shared interface

MySchool uses role- and page-based permissions so staff, students, and guardians do not share the same experience or level of access.

  • Role-based permissions

    Access follows assigned roles and granted permissions under school administration control.

  • Separated experiences

    Staff workspaces are distinct from student and guardian portals.

  • Branch-aware access

    Where branches are used, access can be organized according to school structure.
