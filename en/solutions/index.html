School management

A clearer view of day-to-day school operations

School information is often scattered across departments, spreadsheets, and separate tools — making it harder for leadership to follow what is happening.

MySchool gives management a dashboard, an action center, and granular permissions — with visibility into students, academics, finance, HR, and reports according to granted access.

  • Dashboard & action center

    Follow what needs leadership attention each school day.

  • Cross-department visibility

    Organized access to students, academics, finance, and HR.

  • Permission control

    Decide what each role can see and manage.

  • Branch management

    Manage school branches in the same platform when needed.
